# E-Life

A choice-driven life simulator about growing up, work, relationships, money, and the moments that change a year.

## Play

Play the latest version on [GitHub Pages](https://bennett-caleb.github.io/E-Life/).

Open `index.html` in a browser. No install or build step is required. Your story saves in the browser you play in.

Start at age 0 with **Be born**. Each life gets a random name, gender, appearance, birthplace, and family. Actions are unlimited within a year; age up when you're ready.

School, libraries, college, entrance tests, and trade/professional certifications build intelligence and qualifications. At 18, apply for jobs: hiring chances depend on intelligence, education, and required exams. Choose routine, physical, dental, vision, hearing, skin, sports, wellness, or bloodwork exams; results can be reassuring or uncover a condition with follow-up choices. Pelvic exams are optional and require consent. Illnesses, doctor visits, and surgeries also affect health. The style category has age-gated outfits with distinct looks and descriptions; each changes the illustrated portrait and stays in the closet. Random story events add dialogue and choices throughout school, social life, and work. Later choices include dating, adoption and parenting, pets, phones and social media, volunteering, licensed car shopping, and homes with different prices. At the end of a life, the legacy report rates kindness, relationships, care, and community choices.
